# Yuma, AZ Garage Door Competitor Inventory and SEO Weakness Assessment (as of Oct 2026)

Method note: Research used web search plus direct fetches of company websites and directories. Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Porch, Thumbtack and TheBuilderMarket returned HTTP 403 or empty pages to the fetch tool. Google Maps star ratings and review counts could not be fetched directly. **Most review counts below are therefore UNVERIFIED.** The AZ ROC license lookup was not reachable through these tools, so licenses are listed only where a company publishes one. "Years in business" figures come from Yellow Pages or from the companies themselves; they are not independently verified.

## Which companies serve Yuma? (inventory: name, URL, phone, address, years, services)

### Takeaway
About 6–8 real local operators serve Yuma. The anchors are Yuma Overhead Door (since 1969), Guaranteed Door Service (a multi-location AZ company), On Track Overhead Doors, Arizona Overhead Doors (an Amarr dealer) and Affordable Garage Doors. Around them sits a large layer of fake-local lead-gen sites and directory listings (A1 Garage Door Service, yumadoorrepair.com, yumagaragedoorandrepair.com, and a cluster of keyword-named "928-259-25xx" listings). No Precision Door Service franchise was found in Yuma.

- Lowe's and Home Depot capture some installation demand from shoppers who are already buying a door.

## Review and directory presence (Google, Yelp, BBB, Angi/HomeAdvisor/Thumbtack)

### Takeaway
Review-platform data was largely unfetchable. What could be seen shows thin directory coverage. BBB's Yuma "doors" category is filled with out-of-area California and Phoenix firms, and Thumbtack's Yuma door results show handymen rather than garage door specialists. This means even modest review volume and directory optimization by a new entrant would stand out.

### Cited Findings
- Yelp pages exist for Yuma Overhead Door and for ABC Garage Door Services & Repairs. Ratings could not be fetched (403). — [Yelp Yuma Overhead](https://www.yelp.com/biz/yuma-overhead-door-yuma-2); [Yelp ABC](https://www.yelp.com/biz/abc-garage-door-services-and-repairs-yuma-2)

- Guaranteed Door Service has a BBB profile (Tucson BBB, ROC 123431) and HomeAdvisor listings that claim a BBB A+ rating (self-reported). — [BBB](https://www.bbb.org/tucson/quote/request-guaranteed-door-service-inc-18246); [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/c.Doors.yuma.AZ.-12024.html)
- Thumbtack Yuma door results: Ganonis Heritage Handyman, 4.8 from 6 reviews (a general handyman). ### Inferences
- Review platforms other than Google are thin in Yuma. Google reviews are probably the main battleground, and Yuma Overhead Door is likely the review leader if the 370 figure is correct.
- A new entrant could own BBB accreditation, Thumbtack and Angi in the garage door category with little effort.

### Gaps
- Google, Yelp and Angi ratings and counts for On Track, Arizona Overhead, Affordable, Guaranteed, S.O.S. and ABC were not verified. Manual Google Maps checks are recommended.

## SEO weaknesses and strengths per competitor

### Takeaway
None of the competitors examined has Spanish-language pages, a blog or FAQ, or a full set of city-specific service pages backed by a real business. Real locals have thin or outdated sites, or none at all. Lead-gen sites have city pages but fake NAP data and no trust signals.

### Cited Findings
- **On Track Overhead Doors**. Strengths: real address, 24/7 line, commercial and produce-cooler niche page, brand logos (Clopay, LiftMaster), a "Se Habla Español" mention, a testimonials page and a showroom. Weaknesses: no Spanish pages, no blog or FAQ, no city pages, no ROC number shown, and a static .html site (likely dated). — [ontrackyuma.com](https://www.ontrackyuma.com/)
- **Affordable Garage Doors**. Strengths: ranks organically, shows ROC 346108, long hours and 24/7, Google Guarantee badge. Weaknesses: inconsistent brand name and years claims, residential only, no city list, no brands, no Spanish, no blog or FAQ, no copyright year. — [affordablegaragedoorsandrepair.com](https://www.affordablegaragedoorsandrepair.com/)
- **Yuma Overhead Door**. Strengths: tenure since 1969 and possibly the largest Google review base (unverified 4.8/370). Weakness: no website found, so it cannot rank organically or for commercial terms. — [Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com/yuma-az/garage-doors-openers); [TheBuilderMarket](https://thebuildermarket.com/us/az/yuma/doors)
- **Guaranteed Door Service**. Strengths: scale (six AZ locations, 30+ trucks), ROC license, BBB profile, 24/7, gates. Weakness: no Yuma-specific location page found, and the brand is Tucson-centric. — [BBB](https://www.bbb.org/tucson/quote/request-guaranteed-door-service-inc-18246)
- **Arizona Overhead Doors**. Strength: Amarr dealer status. Site content not retrievable, so other SEO factors were not assessed. — [azoverhead.com](https://azoverhead.com/)
- **A1 Garage Door Service**. Strengths: domain authority, a dedicated /yuma/ page, promos. Weaknesses: only a Phoenix address, no local proof, no Spanish, marketing superlatives. — [a1garage.com/yuma](https://a1garage.com/yuma/)
- **Lead-gen sites** (yumadoorrepair.com, yumagaragedoorandrepair.com). Strengths: city pages for Foothills, Somerton, San Luis and Wellton, plus keyword targeting. Weaknesses: placeholder addresses and phones, fake testimonials, no license, no Spanish. — [yumadoorrepair.com](https://yumadoorrepair.com/); [yumagaragedoorandrepair.com](https://www.yumagaragedoorandrepair.com/)
- **S.O.S., ABC, Smith, JJR, the Somerton shops**: no websites found. — [Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com/yuma-az/garage-doors-openers)

### Inferences
- Schema markup and page speed were not measured (no tooling). Given the templates seen (static HTML, Elegant Themes), weak or missing LocalBusiness schema is likely across the board. This should be checked with Rich Results Test and PageSpeed before claiming it.

### Gaps
- Schema, Core Web Vitals and backlink profiles were not measured.

## Gaps in the Yuma market (opportunities)

### Takeaway
The clearest openings are: (1) a genuinely bilingual Spanish/English site and GBP; (2) commercial, agricultural and cold-storage dock and cooler doors for Yuma's produce industry; (3) government and military work (City of Yuma IDIQ, MCAS Yuma, YPG); (4) city pages for Somerton, San Luis, Wellton and Foothills backed by real service; and (5) desert heat and dust content.

### Cited Findings
- **Spanish**: no competitor has Spanish pages. On Track only says "Se Habla Español." The Spanish query returns no Yuma garage door company. — [ontrackyuma.com](https://www.ontrackyuma.com/); [search](https://www.thumbtack.com/az/yuma/garage-door-repair)

### Inferences
- No competitor content was found targeting MCAS Yuma, Yuma Proving Ground, base housing, or federal and SAM.gov work. This is a niche for a licensed, insured entrant (an inference; base procurement was not researched).
- San Luis and Somerton are majority Hispanic and border-adjacent. Spanish city pages plus a Spanish GBP description and Spanish review responses would face near-zero competition.
- Winter visitors in Foothills and Fortuna Foothills (inference) could justify "snowbird" home-check and opener/smart-lock content.

### Gaps
- MCAS Yuma and YPG procurement channels and incumbents were not researched.
- The specific cold-storage and produce-shed operators and their door vendors were not identified.
