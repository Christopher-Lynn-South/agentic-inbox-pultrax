Research date: 2026-10-10. About 22 tool calls. Several primary sources (MAG PDF, AZ ROC classification PDF, OEO site) returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so some figures come from secondary aggregators. Each one is flagged where that applies.

## 1. Main markets: population and housing stats, plus new-construction hotspots

### Takeaway
Arizona had about 7.62M residents at July 1, 2025. Population is heavily concentrated in the Phoenix metro (well over 5M) and Tucson (about 0.55M city; about 1.1M+ metro, not verified this session). The fastest growth, and so the most new-home and install demand, is in the West Valley (Buckeye, Goodyear, Surprise) and in Pinal County / the far East Valley (Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, Maricopa, Casa Grande). Core cities like Chandler, Scottsdale and Tucson are close to flat, so demand there is mostly repair and replacement.

## 4. Arizona-specific factors (climate, HOA, ROC licensing, bilingual)

### Takeaway
Licensing: Arizona requires an ROC license for any job over the $1,000 handyman exemption (labor plus materials, no permit) or for any job requiring a permit. Unlicensed exempt workers must advertise "not a licensed contractor." The exact garage door classification could not be verified from an official ROC source. A secondary source says CR-6 (dual) / C-6 (commercial), with a residential equivalent. Climate (extreme heat, monsoon/haboob wind and dust) and a large Spanish-speaking population (about 44% Spanish at home in Yuma city) are real differentiators for content.

### Cited Findings
- **Handyman exemption:** minor work under $1,000 in total labor plus materials that needs no building permit is exempt from ROC licensing (A.R.S. § 32-1121(A)(14)). Exceeding it is a class 1 misdemeanor (A.R.S. § 32-1164). Exempt persons must include "not a licensed contractor" in all advertising — [AZ Commerce Authority handyman checklist](https://www.azcommerce.com/small-business/checklist-items/additional-business-occupations-and-activities/handyman/); [ContractorLicenseRequirements.com (2026)](https://contractorlicenserequirements.com/arizona/handyman-license-requirements/); [HomeGnome](https://homegnome.com/blog/pro-resources/handyman-license-requirements-arizona/)
- Bills proposed raising the exemption to $2,000-$2,500 (e.g. SB 1715 in the 56th Legislature: $1,000 → $2,500). Passage was not confirmed — [azleg.gov SB1715 summary](https://www.azleg.gov/legtext/56leg/1R/summary/S.1715COM.DOCX.htm). A 2026 bill, SB1670, was vetoed. Its relevance to ROC was not determined — [azleg.gov SB1670 vetoed](https://www.azleg.gov/legtext/57leg/2R/summary/H.SB1670_071526_VETOED.DOCX.htm)
- **R-62 Minor Home Improvement** license: covers jobs up to $5,000 including labor and material — [Tucson.com](https://tucson.com/life-entertainment/article_0eb743b0-6626-11ef-9ecd-73739737beda.html)
- **License structure:** the ROC issues residential (R-/KB-), commercial (C-/B-) and dual (CR-/KA/KB) classifications. Dual specialty codes run CR-1 through CR-80. Commercial is "anything other than residential" — [SuretyBondsDirect classification list](https://www.suretybondsdirect.com/blog/arizona-contractors-license-classifications); [ROC License Classification Requirements PDF](https://www.azroc.gov/sites/default/files/2023-11/License%20Classification%20Requirements_6.8.20_0.pdf) (403 on fetch)
- **Garage door classification (unverified):** one broker site says garage door work falls under "CR-6" (dual specialty) and commercial "C-6", requiring a qualifying party with a trade exam and 4+ years of experience. It also cites an "L-37R" code that does not appear in ROC lists — [CT Acquisitions](https://www.ctacquisitions.com/sell-your-business/garage-doors/arizona/). Treat this as unconfirmed and verify at roc.az.gov.
- **Climate and operations:** Yuma growers shift to night harvesting as temperatures rise (Feb 2026), which illustrates the extreme heat — [AZFamily](https://www.azfamily.com/2026/02/27/racing-against-heat-yumas-lettuce-farmers-battle-extreme-temperatures/). A Phoenix company press release (2026) attributes rising repair demand to heat strain on garage systems — [KDH News PR, Southwest Garage Doors](https://kdhnews.com/online_features/press_releases/phoenix-home-garage-system-strain-increases-southwest-garage-doors/article_f07250fa-fffe-5e7c-afe2-8c6311c2cc2f.html) (company claim).
- **Bilingual:** in Yuma city (2020-2024 ACS, age 5+), 41,294 of 92,865 speak Spanish at home (about 44%). In the Yuma County–Yuma City PUMA, 48.3% of households speak a non-English language at home, mostly Spanish (about 49,655 households). The La Paz & Yuma PUMA is 62.4% Hispanic — [DataUSA Yuma PUMA](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/79500US0401101); [DataUSA La Paz & Yuma PUMA](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/79500US0400701)

### Inferences
- Content angles unique to Arizona: insulated (R-value) doors for garages that hit 120°F+; opener motor and logic-board failures in heat; battery-backup openers (required in California by SB 969, but Arizona has no such law, so frame it as a recommendation); UV and heat-cracked bottom seals and weatherstripping; dust and haboob grit in tracks and rollers; monsoon wind-load (bowed panels, off-track doors, struts); HOA-approved colors and styles in master-planned communities (Verrado, Eastmark, Estrella, Vistancia, San Tan Heights, etc.).
- License display: show the ROC license number on every page and GBP (standard practice in Arizona). Most door replacements exceed $1,000, so a license is mandatory for install work.
- Spanish pages make the most sense in Yuma, Somerton, San Luis, west Phoenix (Maryvale), Avondale, Tolleson, south Tucson and Casa Grande. Statewide Hispanic share is about 32% from general knowledge; it was not verified this session.

## 6. Scalable site architecture and when to open real GBPs

### Takeaway
Use a state hub → region (Phoenix West Valley, East Valley, Tucson/Southern AZ, Northern AZ, River Corridor, Yuma) → city structure. Only publish city pages with genuinely unique local content (local proof, jobs, photos, HOAs and neighborhoods, reviews, FAQs). Google treats large sets of near-identical geo pages as doorway abuse. Google Business Profiles may only be created for real, staffed or base locations, not virtual offices. Each additional GBP needs its own staff and service area, and service areas should generally stay within about 2 hours' drive of the base.

### Cited Findings
- Google Business Profile guidelines: a service-area business (SAB) gets one profile for its central office or base. Separate locations with their own service areas and staff can each have a profile. Service areas "generally shouldn't extend beyond about 2 hours of driving" from the base. P.O. boxes, remote mailboxes and virtual offices are ineligible. Co-working spaces need signage, on-site staff and service during hours. SABs operating from home should hide the address. One profile per location — [Google Business Profile Help: Guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)
- Doorway pages are large sets of similar pages targeting geographic variations mainly to capture traffic. Red flags: same office, staff, hours and NAP across dozens of URLs, and templates with only the city swapped. Legitimate SAB city pages carry unique local content and LocalBusiness schema with `areaServed` — [StoreRocket](https://storerocket.io/learn/doorway-pages); [Front-End Checklist service-area rule](https://frontendchecklist.io/rules/seo/service-area); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/doorway-pages/)
- Ads pointing to destinations removed by a manual action get disapproved. One vendor claims the March 2024 core update hit scaled doorway content (unverified) — [Custom Legal Marketing](https://custom.legal/practice-areas/personal-injury-law-firm-marketing/avoiding-doorway-page-penalties/)
- Competitor pattern: A1 uses state-level hub URLs (/areas-served/{state}-garage-door-repair-installation-near-you/) — [a1garage.com](https://www.a1garage.com/). Precision uses /locations/{state}/{city}/{city} franchise pages — [precisiondoor.net](https://www.precisiondoor.net/locations/colorado/colorado-springs/colorado-springs)

### Inferences
- **The 2-hour rule matters for Yuma.** A Yuma-based GBP can realistically cover Yuma County (Yuma, Somerton, San Luis, Wellton, Fortuna Foothills) and arguably Imperial County, CA (out of scope). Phoenix (about 3 hr) and Tucson (about 3.5-4 hr) need separate staffed bases (shop, warehouse or tech home-base with real staff) before a GBP is created. A suggested sequence:
  1. Yuma (existing).
  2. West Valley base (Goodyear/Buckeye/Avondale), covering West Valley plus Phoenix.
  3. East Valley/Pinal base (Mesa/Gilbert/Queen Creek/San Tan Valley/Maricopa), often justified by drive times.
  4. Tucson base (Tucson/Marana/Oro Valley/Sahuarita, plus Casa Grande).
  5. Optional Mohave base (Lake Havasu/Bullhead/Kingman) or Prescott/Flagstaff.
- **Suggested URL architecture:**
  - `/arizona/` hub
  - `/arizona/phoenix-metro/` (west-valley, east-valley), `/arizona/tucson/`, `/arizona/yuma/`, `/arizona/northern-arizona/`, `/arizona/colorado-river/`
  - `/arizona/phoenix-metro/buckeye/garage-door-repair/`
  - Service pages: `/services/garage-door-spring-repair/` etc.
  - Commercial section: `/commercial/loading-dock-door-repair/` with a regional variant only where staffed
  - Spanish mirror `/es/` for Yuma, Phoenix-west and Tucson with hreflang
- **Publishing order for city pages:** launch city pages only where you can serve within a stated response time and can add unique proof (job photos, reviews from that city, HOA notes, local climate issues). Start with about 15-25 high-value cities, not 100+ thin pages. Add pages as each market gets real jobs and reviews.
