# SEO + GEO Playbook for a New Garage Door / Commercial Overhead Door Entrant (2025-2026 evidence)

Research date: 2026-10-10. About 18 tool calls. Many figures come from SEO vendors or agencies and are flagged as such. Where a claim has no source, it is listed under Gaps, not presented as fact.

## Google local pack / Maps ranking factors (categories, proximity, reviews, photos, posts, LSAs, citations, links)

### Takeaway
In Whitespark's 2026 survey (published Nov 2025), the top Maps factors are the primary GBP category, proximity, keywords in the business name and an address in the searched city. Reviews and behavioural signals gained weight, while citations kept declining. A new entrant can control the category, its real address and city, its reviews, and its predefined services. It cannot control proximity, and keyword-stuffing the name is a guideline violation, especially risky in a spam-heavy vertical.

### Cited Findings
- The Whitespark 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors report was published 2025-11-06 (Darren Shaw). It is a survey of 47 experts scoring 187 factors across four areas: local pack/Maps, local organic, conversion and, new for 2026, AI search visibility. — [Whitespark checklist](https://whitespark.ca/local-seo-checklist/); [Whitespark LSRF](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- The 2026 top 10 local pack/Maps factors (score):
  1. Primary GBP Category (227)
  2. Proximity of address to searcher (225)
  3. Keywords in GBP business title (223)
  4. Physical address in city of search (213)
  5. Business open at time of search (189, new)
  6. High numerical Google rating (181)
  7. Address shown on GBP, not SAB (176)
  8. Additional GBP categories (173)
  9. Quantity of native Google reviews with text (170)
  10. Proper map pin placement (165)

  — [Whitespark LSRF 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- Presence of GBP predefined services ranked #22, a new factor that Shaw says his own testing confirmed. — [Whitespark LSRF 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- Shaw suggests the "address shown vs SAB" factor may be a Google bug rather than a true ranking factor, since some profiles kept their rankings after hiding the address. — [Whitespark LSRF 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- Trend: review and behavioural signals gained importance; on-page and link signals dropped slightly; social signals returned as a factor group; citations lost about 0.5 percentage points, continuing a multi-year decline. Scores are expert opinion, not measured data. — [Whitespark LSRF 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- Top LSA (Local Services Ads) factors per Whitespark 2026: budget and bidding, reviews, service selection and response times. Review recency also matters. — [Whitespark LSRF 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- LSA / Google Guaranteed screening (older sources, 2017-2022; verify against current Google help pages):
  - Pinkerton background checks on the owner and on every field employee or subcontractor who enters homes.
  - License verification where required, plus a general liability insurance certificate.
  - Guarantee claims within 30 days of service. Sources conflict on the guarantee cap: up to the invoice amount vs a $2,000 lifetime cap (older policy).

  — [Marketing360](https://www.marketing360.com/paid-search/this-is-how-you-become-a-google-guaranteed-business-on-home-service-ads/); [Duct Tape Marketing](https://www.ducttapemarketing.com/google-local-services/)
- Garage door LSA cost per lead (all agency-sourced, directional only):
  - $25-45 per lead, citing a "2025 Garage Door Industry Market Report".
  - A Jan-Apr 2026 sample of 12 contractors: $49/lead, 38% book rate, $198 per paying customer, vs $173/lead for non-branded Google Ads.
  - Residential repair LSA leads at $15-35, with commercial overhead door leads costing "much higher".

  — [Emulent](https://emulent.com/resources/trends/garage-door-industry-marketing-trends-and-projections/); [Elevarus](https://elevarus.com/garage-door-google-ads-cpl-benchmarks-by-job-type/); [Pipelineon](https://pipelineon.com/blog/garage-door-google-ads-guide/); [Enrich Labs](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/garage-door-marketing-complete-guide-2026)
- For contrast, LocaliQ/WordStream's broader "Doors & Windows" search-ads benchmark is about $200 per lead. This is not comparable to LSA. — summarized via search of [Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/garage-doors-and-windows-tracking-and-analytics-statistics) (secondary)

### Inferences
- Primary category is the single biggest lever under the owner's control.
  - For a repair-led business, "Garage door repair service" likely matches the money queries ("garage door repair near me") better than "Garage door supplier".
  - Add "Garage door supplier" and installer-type and commercial categories as secondaries.
  - Test by swapping the primary category and watching grid-rank tools for 2-4 weeks.
  - This is practitioner reasoning; I found no garage-door-specific category test.
- Because proximity and address-in-city rank so high, a new SAB at one location will rank strongly only near its verified address. Expect to win Maps in a radius around the real base, and use LSAs, organic city pages and AI visibility to reach farther.
- "Open at time of search" (#5) favours genuine 24/7 or extended hours, a real advantage in emergency garage door repair. Set hours honestly; staffing a phone line around the clock is required for it to pay off.
- Fill every GBP predefined service: spring replacement, opener repair, cable, off-track, panel replacement, new door installation, commercial rolling steel, sectional overhead doors, dock equipment and so on.
- LSAs are likely the fastest route to top-of-page visibility for a new entrant. LSA ranking depends on budget, reviews and responsiveness, not on organic history.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve Whitespark's group-weight percentages; they sit in a chart image.
- I found no 2025-2026 controlled test on "Garage door supplier" vs "Garage door repair service" as primary category.
- My Sterling Sky / Joy Hawkins garage door research search returned only an A1 Garage Door case-study page, which loaded empty, so no specific claims can be attributed to it.
- No 2025-2026 evidence gathered on GBP posts, Q&A (reportedly being phased out in favour of AI answers, unverified here) or photo counts.
- I did not confirm current (2026) LSA guarantee terms from Google's own pages.

## Service-area business rules, verification, and spam in the garage door niche

### Takeaway
Garage door and locksmith are long-documented spam verticals, and Google has responded with stricter and advanced verification. Video verification is now standard. A new entrant should verify honestly at a real location, hide the address if it does not serve customers there, and keep service areas within about 2 hours' drive and below state level. It can also use spam reporting against fake competitors as a legitimate tactic.

### Cited Findings
- Google's help page on video verification:
  - Available to storefront, service-area and hybrid businesses.
  - The video must be unedited and continuous, at least 30 seconds, recorded and uploaded from a mobile device via the Business Profile.
  - If you don't serve customers at your address, don't show it.

  — [Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705)
- On 2025-06-24 Google updated its service-area guidelines to prohibit countries or states as service areas. This adds to the existing limit of roughly 2 hours' driving time from the base. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-bans-countries-and-states-from-business-profile-service-areas/) (single source)
- Practitioner tips for SAB video verification: show branded vehicles, tools or business cards, and footage of work at customer sites. — [Seoteric](https://www.seoteric.com/google-service-area-business-verification-update-2025-implications-for-local-businesses/) (agency blog)
- Google's own research (older, about 2017) found:
  - Fake contractors requested hundreds of postcard verifications to non-existent suites or PO boxes.
  - Google responded by prohibiting bulk registrations at most addresses and piloting advanced verification for locksmiths and plumbers.

  — [Google Research blog](https://research.google/blog/keeping-fake-listings-off-google-maps/?m=1)
- In a Search Engine Land analysis of abusive listings, 25.7% were locksmiths and 40.3% were on-call industries. The authors note this may reflect heavier enforcement rather than more spam. (Older data, about 2017.) — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/?p=272913)
- A Wall Street Journal investigation (2019, via WARC) found scam listings concentrate in contractors and repair services used in emergencies, including hijacked legitimate business names with substituted phone numbers. — [WARC](https://cdn.warc.com/newsandopinion/news/millions_of_fake_businesses_list_on_google_maps/42261)
- A CBC Marketplace investigation found more than 80 fake Toronto-area listings, and one expert estimated up to 85% of home-services listings could be fake. That figure is one person's opinion, not measured. — [Local Search Forum](https://localsearchforum.com/threads/marketplace-uncovers-more-than-80-fake-toronto-area-listings-on-google-maps.55569/latest); [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/news/canada/locksmith-google-maps-reviews-fake-marketplace-1.5417931)
- Legitimate operators in high-spam verticals also get caught in suspensions. — [SEO Locale](https://seolocale.com/why-locksmiths-arent-trusted-on-google-business-profile/) (agency blog)

### Inferences
- Do not use virtual offices, UPS boxes or co-working addresses to create extra GBPs in target cities. In a vertical with advanced verification and a heavy spam history, this is the most likely path to suspension.
- Expansion to new cities should come from real staffed locations, a yard or warehouse, which commercial overhead door work often justifies anyway.
- Name the GBP exactly as the legal or DBA name. If a keyword-rich name is desired (for example "X Garage Door Repair"), register it as the actual DBA and put it on vehicles and signage first. Keyword stuffing invites competitor edits and suspensions.
- Competitors with fake or keyword-stuffed listings can be reported through Google's Business Redressal Complaint Form. Practitioners widely use this in spam-heavy verticals, but I did not verify current form behaviour in this session.
- Prepare a documentation kit before verification and any reinstatement:
  - business license, Arizona contractor license (check Arizona ROC requirements separately), insurance
  - branded vehicle and tools
  - utility bill at the base address

### Gaps
- I found no 2025-2026 quantitative study specific to garage door spam. Joy Hawkins / Sterling Sky material could not be retrieved.
- I did not verify the current Business Redressal Complaint Form or the reinstatement process.
- Whether garage door now falls under Google's "advanced verification" category alongside locksmiths and plumbers is unconfirmed.

## On-site SEO: service pages, city pages, schema, FAQ, pricing, Spanish, Core Web Vitals

### Takeaway
For local organic and AI visibility, the 2026 survey's #1 factor is a dedicated page per service. City or geo relevance, quality links, niche focus and GBP landing-page title and headings also rank high. City pages are fine only when each carries distinct, real local value; templated city-swap pages are doorway spam under Google's policy.

### Cited Findings
- The 2026 top 10 local organic factors:
  1. Dedicated page for each service (210)
  2. Geographic keyword relevance of content (190)
  3. Inbound link quality/authority (187)
  4. Keywords in GBP landing page title tag (179)
  5. Links from industry-relevant domains (175)
  6. Internal linking (174)
  7. Topical keyword relevance sitewide (172)
  8. Keywords in GBP landing page headings (169)
  9. Website's niche focus (169)
  10. Keywords in inbound anchor text (169)

  — [Whitespark LSRF 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- Google's spam policy defines doorway pages to include large sets of similar pages targeting geographic variations that funnel users to substantially similar destinations. Google has warned about city/state landing pages since 2008, again in 2015, and John Mueller cautioned in 2019 against 1,300 city pages. — [Search Engine Roundtable](https://seroundtable.com/archives/017725.html); [Local Search Forum](https://localsearchforum.com/threads/google-city-landing-pages-can-be-doorway-pages-against-guidelines.55438/); [StoreRocket](https://storerocket.io/learn/doorway-pages)
- Red flags: swapping only the city name in the title, H1 and intro; identical NAP and contact form across dozens of URLs. Safer pattern: unique local case studies, area-specific FAQs, and LocalBusiness schema with `areaServed`. — [Splinternet/LinkedIn summary](https://cz.linkedin.com/in/petrpinkas); [Front-End Checklist](https://frontendchecklist.io/rules/seo/service-area)
- In the AI search factors, "Scannable content structure across entire website" ranked #10 and "Dedicated page for each service" #2. — [Whitespark LSRF 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)

### Inferences
- Site architecture:
  - One strong page per service, split residential vs commercial: broken spring, opener repair and install, cables and rollers, off-track, new door install, insulated doors, commercial sectional, rolling steel and roll-up, fire doors, dock levelers, preventive maintenance contracts.
  - A small number of genuinely distinct city pages, each with local job photos and case studies, neighbourhood-specific issues (for Arizona: heat, monsoon dust, UV damage to weatherseals, insulated doors), local reviews and real response times.
- Make the GBP landing page (usually the homepage, or a city page for a second location) carry the primary service and city in the title tag and H1. These are survey factors #4 and #8.
- Schema: LocalBusiness subtype HomeAndConstructionBusiness (or the more specific types in Schema.org's hierarchy), with `areaServed`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `sameAs` pointing to all profiles, `aggregateRating` only per Google's self-serving review rules, and Service markup. Schema is not a confirmed Google ranking factor; its main value is entity clarity for AI systems.
- A transparent pricing page (spring replacement ranges, service call fee, opener install ranges) is likely a strong GEO asset, because AI answers to "how much does garage door spring repair cost in Phoenix" need citable numbers. This follows from the GEO paper's statistics finding in the next section.
- Spanish pages make sense for Arizona's large Spanish-speaking population. Use real translations with hreflang, not machine-spun duplicates. I found no direct ranking study for this.
- Core Web Vitals: I found no 2025-2026 evidence in this session quantifying their local ranking impact. Treat them as a conversion and UX hygiene item.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 data was gathered on Core Web Vitals impact, Spanish-page performance, pricing-page impact or FAQ schema. Google restricted FAQ rich results in 2023; that is background knowledge not re-verified here.

## Generative Engine Optimization: how ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews/AI Mode, Gemini, Copilot and Claude pick local businesses

### Takeaway
AI engines lean on brand-controlled sources (website, listings), directories and review sites (Yelp especially for Perplexity; Porch, Angi, Facebook and Yelp in the Bing data ChatGPT uses), and third-party "best of" lists. In Whitespark's 2026 survey, the top AI factor is presence on expert-curated best-of lists, and "mentions are the new links". Being recommended by ChatGPT is much harder than ranking in Google's local pack.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 top 10 AI search visibility factors:
  1. Presence on expert-curated best-of lists (179)
  2. Dedicated page per service (170)
  3. Prominence on key industry-relevant domains (167)
  4. Quality/authority of unstructured citations (160)
  5. Authority of third-party sites hosting reviews (156)
  6. Geographic keyword relevance (153)
  7. Quantity of unstructured citations (147)
  8. High Google ratings (146)
  9. Niche focus (138)
  10. Scannable content structure (137)

  Shaw: "In AI SEO, mentions (citations) are the new link." — [Whitespark LSRF 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- BrightLocal (July 2025) ran 20 searches across 10 niches on Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity and ChatGPT Search:
  - All LLMs used directories and citations.
  - Yelp was a source in 33% of searches; Perplexity used Yelp in every industry tested; Gemini was the only one not citing Yelp directly.
  - AI Mode relied heavily on GBP. Business websites were the majority of sources overall.
  - ChatGPT drew on niche directories (for example ten dental directories for dentist queries).
  - AI Overviews and home services were not covered.

  — [BrightLocal](https://brightlocal.com/blog/ai-search-using-listings-sources)
- An earlier BrightLocal ChatGPT study (about Dec 2024, 800 local searches) found ChatGPT Search sources were business websites 58%, business mentions 27% and directories 15%. — [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/local-seo/uncovering-chatgpt-search-sources/)
- Yext (Oct 2025) studied 6.8M citations across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity (Jul-Aug 2025): 86% came from brand-managed sources such as websites and listings. Yext sells listings management, so it has an incentive here. — [Business Wire / Yext](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251009106549/en); [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/35338737/yext-research-86-of-ai-citations-come-from-brand-managed-sources-clarifying-how-marketers-can-compete-in-the-ai-search-era)
- SOCi's 2026 Local Visibility Index (more than 350k locations, as summarized in search results) found:
  - ChatGPT local recommendations are about 30 times harder to earn than Google local rankings.
  - Fewer than half of Google local leaders appear in AI recommendations.
  - Recommended businesses average about 4.3 stars on ChatGPT, 4.1 on Perplexity and 3.9 on Gemini.

  I did not read this primary document; numbers come from a search summary. — [SOCi](https://www.soci.ai/insights/genai-local-search-citations) (related)
- SOCi (Aug 2024, older) found:
  - Perplexity cited Yelp among its first three sources nearly every time.
  - ChatGPT, when it searched, used Bing and often cited local blogs and listicles.
  - Bing Copilot cited websites and Yelp.
  - Gemini mostly cited business websites for local services.
  - "Local services" averaged only 4.2 listings per AI answer, so it is a competitive category.

  — [SOCi](https://www.soci.ai/insights/genai-local-search-citations)
- Whitespark (Sept 2025) studied 153 queries across 17 categories in 9 US cities, looking at the review sources shown in Bing Places:
  - Facebook is the most common review source overall, ahead of Yelp.
  - Porch is the leading review source for garage door repair; Porch, Yellowpages and Angi are notable for home services.
  - ChatGPT has historically used Bing web and Bing Places data, though not exclusively; some claim it now uses Google, with no consensus.
  - Recommendation: update or claim Bing Places and build reviews on the platforms that appear in your category.

  — [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/blog/want-to-rank-in-chatgpt-focus-on-these-review-sites-new-research/)
- Data providers behind ChatGPT local results:
  - Vendor blogs claim Foursquare supplies about 60-70% or more of ChatGPT's local business data. This is unconfirmed by OpenAI or Foursquare.
  - Peec.ai reports ChatGPT search uses its own index plus at least eight external providers.

  — [Local Falcon](https://www.localfalcon.com/blog/chatgpt-local-search-data-sources-where-does-business-info-come-from); [Map Atlas](https://mapatlas.eu/blog/chatgpt-local-business-data-sources-foursquare); [Peec.ai](https://docs.peec.ai/research/chatgpt-search-result-providers)
- Ahrefs' AI brand-visibility correlation study (about 75k brands, reported mostly via secondary coverage):
  - Branded web mentions correlate 0.664 with AI Overview presence vs 0.218 for backlinks.
  - Ahrefs' own June 2025 post found a weak correlation between domain link metrics and AI mentions.
  - A secondary summary says only 38% of AI Overview citations came from top-10 organic results by March 2026, down from 76% in July 2025.

  Verify these against Ahrefs' primary pages. — [Ahrefs blog](https://ahrefs.com/blog/domain-link-metrics-not-good-mention-predictors/); [Radiant Elephant](https://www.radiantelephant.com/brand-mentions-vs-backlinks-ai-search/) (secondary)
- The GEO paper (Aggarwal et al., arXiv 2311.09735, KDD 2024) found that adding citations, quotations or statistics boosted visibility in generative engine responses by up to 40% on GEO-bench (10k queries), and up to 37% on Perplexity.ai. Per-tactic breakdowns in blogs are unverified. This is 2023 research on general queries, not local. — [Liner review](https://liner.com/review/geo-generative-engine-optimization); [Everything-PR](https://everything-pr.com/we-found-the-paper-that-launched-geo-heres-whats-actually-in-it/)
- Consumer adoption:
  - A 2025 GatherUp survey found 31% of consumers have used conversational AI for local recommendations many times.
  - BrightLocal (2026, via search summary) reports 45% use AI tools for local recommendations. I could not verify the "up from 6%" comparison.

  — [GatherUp](https://gatherup.com/blog/fresh-data-on-how-consumers-are-discovering-local-businesses-in-2025/); [BrightLocal stats](https://brightlocal.com/research/local-seo-statistics/)

### Inferences
- GEO priorities for a garage door entrant, in order:
  1. Get into local "best garage door companies in [city]" listicles: local media, chamber of commerce, Expertise.com/ThreeBest-style curated lists, supplier dealer locators (Clopay, Amarr, Wayne Dalton, LiftMaster, Raynor and similar).
  2. Build complete, consistent profiles on Yelp, Facebook, Porch, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect and Foursquare.
  3. Add a dedicated page per service with citable numbers: price ranges, spring cycle ratings, response times.
  4. Earn unstructured mentions: local news, sponsorships, HOA newsletters, Reddit and Nextdoor recommendations earned organically.
- Ratings matter for AI inclusion: recommended businesses average about 4.1-4.3 stars, and "High Google ratings" is a top-10 AI factor. A 4.7+ average across multiple platforms is a reasonable target.
- llms.txt: I found no evidence that any major AI engine uses it for local recommendations. It is low priority and low cost.
- Track AI visibility by regularly running prompts ("best garage door repair in Mesa", "who fixes commercial roll-up doors in Phoenix") across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Mode and Copilot, since answers vary run to run.

## Bing Places and Apple Business Connect

### Takeaway
Bing Places matters mainly because ChatGPT has historically pulled Bing web and Places data, and Copilot uses it. Its exact current role is disputed. It is cheap to claim, so do it. I found no 2025-2026 quantitative evidence on Apple Business Connect's effect.

### Cited Findings
- ChatGPT has historically used Bing for web search and Bing Places for local results, not exclusively. Whitespark recommends refreshing or creating Bing Places listings. Some argue ChatGPT may now use Google; there is no consensus. — [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/blog/want-to-rank-in-chatgpt-focus-on-these-review-sites-new-research/)
- Bing Copilot showed maps and local packs and cited websites and Yelp (2024 data). — [SOCi](https://www.soci.ai/insights/genai-local-search-citations)
- Foursquare is claimed by vendors as a major ChatGPT local data source (unconfirmed). — [Map Atlas](https://mapatlas.eu/blog/chatgpt-local-business-data-sources-foursquare)

### Inferences
- Claim Bing Places (it can import from GBP), Apple Business Connect (Apple Maps and Siri, relevant to iPhone users) and Foursquare/Swarm business listings, with identical NAP, hours and categories.

### Gaps
- No study was found quantifying Apple Business Connect's impact on Apple Maps, Siri or Apple Intelligence local results.

## Compliant review generation (Google and FTC rules)

### Takeaway
The FTC's Consumer Review rule (in force since Oct 2024) bans fake, AI-generated, insider-undisclosed and sentiment-conditioned paid reviews, with penalties above $50k per violation. Google separately bans review gating and selective solicitation. The safe tactic is to ask every customer, at job completion, through SMS or email with a direct link, and to respond to all reviews.

### Cited Findings
- The FTC rule took effect 2024-10-21. It covers fake reviews, reviews bought on condition of being positive, undisclosed insider reviews, company-controlled "independent" review sites, and review suppression through threats. Incentives conditioned on positive sentiment (for example a discount only for 5 stars) are illegal. — [Momentic Marketing primer](https://momenticmarketing.com/blog/seo-primer-ftc-consumer-reviews); [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/node/327381)
- The maximum civil penalty is cited at $53,088 per violation by the FTC page (inflation-adjusted); another source says $51,744. — [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/node/327381)
- Google's policy prohibits discouraging negative reviews or selectively soliciting positive ones (review gating). The FTC rule covers only some gating forms. — [Momentic Marketing](https://momenticmarketing.com/blog/seo-primer-ftc-consumer-reviews)
- "Quantity of native Google reviews with text" is a top-10 Maps factor, and review signals gained weight in 2026. — [Whitespark LSRF 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)

### Inferences
- Review process:
  - The technician asks in person and triggers an automated SMS with the GBP review link immediately after payment.
  - A follow-up goes out 3 days later.
  - Rotate secondary platforms (Yelp: never solicit, per Yelp policy; Facebook, Porch, Angi, BBB) according to the platforms that surface for garage door in Bing and AI.
- Encourage natural detail ("which service, which city") without scripting the content.
- Do not offer incentives for Google reviews. Google prohibits them, even though the FTC allows disclosed, sentiment-neutral incentives.
- Respond to every review, mentioning the service and city naturally.
- Review velocity matters for LSAs, where recency is a factor.

### Gaps
- Google's exact policy wording on incentives and review gating was not fetched directly in this session.
- Yelp's no-solicitation policy is background knowledge, not verified here.

## Typical timelines and budgets

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous timeline studies. Agency-sourced cost-per-lead benchmarks suggest LSAs ($25-50/lead residential) produce leads within weeks. Maps and organic rankings for a new entrant depend on proximity and reviews and typically take months.

### Cited Findings
- Garage door LSA CPL is about $25-45 (2025 agency report) and $49/lead in a 2026 sample of 12 shops (38% book rate, $198 per customer). Non-branded Google Ads cost $173/lead in the same sample; another agency cites about $91 for paid search vs about $30 for mature SEO. — [Emulent](https://emulent.com/resources/trends/garage-door-industry-marketing-trends-and-projections/); [Elevarus](https://elevarus.com/garage-door-google-ads-cpl-benchmarks-by-job-type/)
- Commercial overhead door leads cost much more than residential repair leads (no figure given). — [Enrich Labs](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/garage-door-marketing-complete-guide-2026)

### Inferences
- Indicative phasing (practitioner judgement, not sourced data):
  - **Month 0-1:** GBP verification, LSA onboarding, citations and profiles (Bing, Apple, Yelp, Facebook, Porch, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Foursquare), core service pages.
  - **Months 1-6:** review engine aiming at a steady weekly flow; city and case-study pages; local PR and best-of list outreach.
  - **Months 6-12+:** organic and Maps gains extend beyond the immediate radius; AI mentions begin to accrue.
- Budget LSAs as the main early lead source, and use organic SEO and GEO to lower blended CPL over time.

### Gaps
- No independent 2025-2026 data was found on SEO timelines or retainer costs for garage door companies.
- No Arizona/Phoenix-specific CPL data was found.
